Electrical Engineering • University of British Columbia

I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia (Expected Graduation 2028, Cumulative GPA: 3.85). My background spans bare-metal embedded firmware, digital hardware architecture on FPGAs, analog circuit design, and parameter-efficient machine learning research.

Currently, I work as an Embedded Software Test Engineer at Stryker, where I contribute to automated test frameworks for medical device hardware, build Python integration test pipelines, and perform electrical verification using oscilloscopes and function generators.

I also lead the Applied AI team at UBC AgroBot, directing a 10-member engineering team developing real-time computer vision suites for an autonomous agricultural rover. Additionally, as a Research Assistant at the Yankai Group (UBC), I conduct experimental research on catastrophic forgetting in parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) methods for large neural architectures.

Areas of Interest: Embedded systems & firmware (C, Assembly), digital logic design (SystemVerilog, FPGAs), robotics & dynamic control loops (PID, Kalman filters), sensor conditioning & PCB prototyping, and machine learning systems.
